Tattoo Service Checklist

Tattoo Inventory

Quantity items count pieces: tap + / − or type the number directly, and the price is per piece. Percentage items track how full a bottle or pack is: type the amount left: 1 = 100%, 0.75 = 75%, or just type 75 for 75%. The app then shows how much money is left and how much was used. LOW = 2 pcs or 20% left. Items with sizes or colors (Cartridge Needles, Ink Cups, Inks, Tattoo Cover): tap the item name to open its list, add sizes or colors, and track each one separately. Start a session so every “−” is added to that session’s cost.
